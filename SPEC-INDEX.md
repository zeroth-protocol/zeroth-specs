## Publication rules

Only material that has passed publication review should be promoted from internal research into this repository.

A document marked **Draft / Normative: No** may describe approved architectural direction while remaining explicitly non-binding. Promotion to normative or release-bound status requires the applicable Zeroth Improvement Proposal, engineering, security, interoperability, and `ZEROTH-OSG-01` gates.

Research prototypes, qualification artifacts, assurance snapshots, and audit targets are not automatically protocol standards.
